Tonight, two legends collide.
On the left, the Black-Eyed Children.
On the right, Cerberus.
Who wins? Comment.
A kid flees through the woods, until a lamp glows in an abandoned cabin.
They slip inside, but a knock comes.
Legend says they cannot enter unless invited.
So the kid stays silent, but the floor begins to glow.
Cerberus, who keeps the gate of the dead, climbs through the boards and blocks the door.
Legend also says music puts him to sleep.
Round one. Three heads snap and hurl them off the porch.
They rise unmarked, and one begins to hum.
Round two. The lullaby drifts, and the left head sags.
Then the middle falls, and Cerberus sinks to his knees.
Only one head stays awake, and the children step closer.
Round three. They speak in the kid's own voice.
But the kid answers.
The song breaks, and all three heads wake.
Cerberus seizes them, and the porch splits open into a red gate.
He drags both children down, and the gate slams shut.
Cerberus wins.
At dawn, he turns to the kid.
But next night, a red glow creeps under the kid's floor.
Then, one knock.
Did hell really keep them?
Who's next?
