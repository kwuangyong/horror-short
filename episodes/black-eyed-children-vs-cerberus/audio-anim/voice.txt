Who survives?
Tonight, two legends collide.
On the left, the Black-Eyed Children.
On the right, Cerberus.
Who wins? Comment.
A kid flees through the woods, until a lamp glows in an abandoned cabin.
They slip inside, but a knock comes.
Legend says they cannot enter unless invited.
So they repeat it, the lamp dies, and the kid's hand drifts toward the latch.
Then the floor glows, and Cerberus, keeper of the gate of the dead, rises.
His tail is a serpent, and his drool withers the grass.
Legend also says music puts him to sleep.
Three heads snap, and the children fly off the porch.
They rise unblinking, humming.
The lullaby drifts, two heads sag, and one fights sleep.
Now they speak in the kid's own voice.
But the kid answers.
The song breaks, and all three heads wake.
Cerberus seizes them, and the porch splits into a red gate.
He drags both children down, and the gate slams shut.
Cerberus wins.
At dawn, he turns to the kid.
But next night, a red glow creeps under the kid's floor.
Then, one knock.
Did hell really keep them?
Who's next?
