## 2. ANTI-GUARDIAN (RIGHT): MEDUSA — Combat Kit

- **Primary Sources (Public Domain)**:
  - Hesiod, *Theogony* (270–281): [Theoi – Gorgones](https://www.theoi.com/Pontios/Gorgones.html) (Medusa as the sole **mortal** Gorgon).
  - Pseudo-Apollodorus, *Bibliotheca* (2.4.2–3): [Perseus Digital Library – Apollodorus 2.4.2](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0022%3Atext%3DLibrary%3Abook%3D2%3Achapter%3D4%3Asection%3D2) (serpent-scaled head & living snakes, boar-like fangs, **brazen/bronze hands**, **golden wings**).
  - Ovid, *Metamorphoses* (Book IV, 770–803): [Theoi – Ovid Metamorphoses 4](https://www.theoi.com/Text/OvidMetamorphoses4.html) (petrifying gaze turning men, Cetus, and Atlas to stone; cursed serpent hair).
