# Hooks, Titles & Description · SCP-096 vs MEDUSA (Episode #007)

## Hooks (0–3 s)
1. **4-Pixel Fugitive Incident -> Clash (Selected):** Fast 3-shot story teaser -> (1) terrified fugitive soldier clutching a crumpled four-pixel photograph stumbles inside mist-choked Greek temple ruins, (2) Medusa's emerald eye turns him to screaming grey stone right as SCP-096 smashes explosively through the rear wall behind him, (3) `"WHO SURVIVES?"` slams over the eye-lock `beam-clash` -> `glitch` rewind into Split-Roll.
2. **Question Hook:** `"A fugitive soldier ran from SCP-096 straight into Medusa's temple... what happens when both curses lock eyes?"`
3. **Photo-to-Stone Hook:** Four glowing pixels fall from a freshly petrified hand — seconds before SCP-096 tears through the marble wall behind Medusa.

## Titles
1. `SCP-096 vs MEDUSA — A Fugitive Led SCP-096 Straight Into Medusa's Temple`
2. `Don't Look at SCP-096... Unless You're MEDUSA`
3. `The Unstoppable Scream vs The Stone Gaze | SCP-096 vs MEDUSA`

## Description
A fugitive soldier who accidentally glimpsed four cursed pixels of SCP-096 flees into Medusa's mist-choked ruins to hide. Medusa freezes him to stone mid-scream — seconds before SCP-096 tears through the temple wall to claim its target. No heroes, no good-guy bias — just two ancient visual curses colliding in a cursed stone garden.
