# Hooks, Titles & Description · SCP-096 vs MEDUSA (Episode #007)

## Hooks (0–3 s)
1. **Visual Curse Clash (Selected):** Extreme close-up of Medusa's emerald eye locking onto SCP-096's unhinged screaming jaw as grey stone creeps across its face → `"WHO SURVIVES?"`
2. **Question Hook:** `"What happens when SCP-096 and Medusa look each other in the eyes?"`
3. **4-Pixel Hook:** Four white pixels drop onto a marble floor surrounded by stone statues — then the wall explodes.

## Titles
1. `SCP-096 vs MEDUSA — What Happens When They Lock Eyes?`
2. `Don't Look at SCP-096... Unless You're MEDUSA`
3. `The Unstoppable Scream vs The Stone Gaze | SCP-096 vs MEDUSA`

## Description
What happens when the creature you must NEVER look at locks eyes with the cursed Gorgon whose gaze turns flesh to stone? No heroes, no mercy — just two ancient visual curses colliding in a mist-shrouded temple.
