## ACT 1 · THE FORBIDDEN AEGEAN ISLAND (0–22 s · Direct Cold Open, No Wheel)
> On a forbidden Aegean *island*, a Foundation squad came to contain a living myth.

[SHOT #2 insert] Tight close-up on the crumpled mountain photograph trembling in his gloved hand — four glowing white pixels (`glint` red).
[SHOT #3 cu] Textbook facial close-up: soldier's panicked face looking over his shoulder as he stumbles behind a broken column (`run` -> `look-back`, `dutch` -5°).

[SHOT #5 ecu at eye] Textbook facial zoom: Medusa's narrowed emerald slit-pupil eyes lock coldly onto the intruder (`glint` emerald).

## ACT 3 · HIGH-SPEED EVASION & CLASH (34–69 s)
### BEAT 1: SUPERSONIC CHARGE vs WINGED BACKFLIP EVADE
> Both curses *ignited* at once, and Zero Nine Six blurred across the courtyard like a missile.
[SHOT #13 wide] 1v1 establishing across the misty Greek columns: SCP-096 LEFT crouching to launch vs Medusa RIGHT spreading her golden wings.
[SHOT #14 follow] Dynamic tracking dash: SCP-096 sprints at blinding speed (`charge`, `speed`), long bloody claws swiping across the frame.
> Medusa fired a petrifying *beam*, then backflipped skyward a split-second before its claws struck.
[SHOT #15 versus] Whip-pan LEFT: Medusa's emerald `beam` grazes 096's shoulder (`stone: 0.35`, `sparks`), yet 096 lunges straight through the glare!

[SHOT #23 cu] Close-up on Medusa pinned beneath the rubble, dark ichor on her cheek, vipers thrashing wildly (`hand`).

### BEAT 3: POINT-BLANK PETRIFICATION (A4 CLIMAX)
> Her serpent coils *pinned*, Medusa stopped dodging and poured her entire curse into one gaze.
[SHOT #24 low] SCP-096 towers over her, blood-drenched jaw wide, raising its hooked claws (`roar`, `hand`).
[SHOT #25 ecu at claw] Tight zoom on 096's bloody claws plunging downward directly above Medusa's writhing snake crown.
[SHOT #26 ecu at eye] Reverse-shot: Medusa's eyes blaze blinding emerald (`gaze-overdrive`, `glint`, emerald `pillar`).
> (medusa's voice, venomous layered hiss) Turn to stone!
[SHOT #27 versus] **FINAL `beam-clash`** at point-blank range: SCP-096's crimson acoustic fury colliding against Medusa's full-power emerald petrification (`sparks`, `shake`).
> Emerald fire *swallowed* the shriek, locking pale flesh into solid rock from the eyes down.
[SHOT #28 versus] `beam-clash` breaks RIGHT->LEFT (`win: 'R'`) with **slow-mo (`0.35`) + crash-zoom** — blinding emerald wave engulfs SCP-096 (`flash`, `impact`, `pillar`).
[SHOT #29 cu] Slow-mo facial zoom: grey granite races across SCP-096's screaming face (`stone: 0.4 -> 1.0`), freezing it solid inches from Medusa's throat (`dust`).

## ACT 4 · AFTERMATH & LIVING MARBLE JUGGERNAUT CLIFFHANGER (69–86 s)
> Breathing hard among the *rubble*, Medusa turned her back on her deadliest trophy.
[SHOT #30 low] Medusa pushes herself upright (`breathe-after`, `wingDmg: 0.75`), golden wing dragging, glancing coldly at the frozen statue of SCP-096.

[SHOT #32 ecu at eye] Beat 2 (**music cuts dead**): Tight facial zoom on the solid stone statue's face — **its granite eyes ignite blazing crimson (`glint` red) and its stone head snaps RIGHT to track Medusa's back!**

> (scp096's voice, dry distorted whisper) You just gave me armor.
