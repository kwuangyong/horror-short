## 4. Hand-Off Notes for Carbon (Rig & FX Checklist)
1. **SCP-682 Rig Flags**: Expose state flags `damage` (`0..1`, charred/torn ribs), `regen` (`0..1`, crimson tendrils + green steam), `armor` (`0..1`, obsidian-chitin plates + bone grounding ridges), `wings` (`0..1`, massive tattered bone-and-membrane wings), and `spikes` (`0..1`, dorsal spike launch recoil).
2. **Pegasus Rig Flags**: Expose `aura` (`0..1`, 3-layer gold/cyan/violet ribbons), `diveFlame` (`0..1`, gold-amber meteor envelope), `stormCharge` (`0..1`, cyan-white-gold lightning crackle on wings/eyes), and `surge` (`0..1`, aqua-gold Hippocrene flash sphere).
3. **180-Degree Combat Rule**: Keep **SCP-682 anchored LEFT** (facing right / angling up-right during aerial phase) and **Pegasus anchored RIGHT** (facing left / diving down-left, then rocketing straight up in Phase 3).
4. **Zero-Beam Audit**: Verify that every ranged interaction uses **forked sky-thunderbolts**, **spinning wind-crescents**, **individual bone-spikes**, or **bubbling acid globs** — zero straight flat-colored energy beams.
