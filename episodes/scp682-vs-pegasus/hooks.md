# Hooks & Titles · Episode #005 (SCP-682 vs Pegasus)

## Titles
1. **SCP-682 Escaped the Lab... Then PEGASUS Dived From Olympus!**
2. **SCP-682 vs PEGASUS — What Happens When the Unkillable Reptile Grows Wings?**

## Hook (0–3 s)
- Visual: Red sirens flash inside a breached SCP lab → steel doors explode open → horrifying scarred SCP-682 erupts into the night forest, clashing mid-air with prismatic-aura Pegasus.
- On-screen text: **"WHO SURVIVES?"** (slam at 2.0 s) → glitch rewind into Split-Roll.
