## 4. Hand-Off Notes for Carbon (Rig & FX Checklist)
1. **SCP-682 Rig Flags (Sequel Extension)**:
   - Default opening state: `armor: 1, wings: 1` (direct continuity from Ep #005).
   - Add `overcharge` (`0..1`, swollen +18–25% scale, stolen cyan arcs + lime acid veins), `shattered` (`0..1`, torn wing-stumps + flying obsidian armor chunks), and `vaporize` (`0..1`, glowing white-gold-amber edge disintegration into ash particles).
   - Separate epilogue prop/rig `boneShard`: single charred bone fragment with pulsing `pulse` (`0..1`, crimson-green heartbeat), writhing `tendrils` (`0..1`), and `eyeOpen` (`0..1`).
2. **Zeus Rig Flags (New Original Rig `zeus`)**:
   - Muscular bare-chested war-king with thick flowing silver beard, golden laurel crown, white-gold himation, and billowing indigo-cyan storm-cloak.
   - Expose `overdrive` (`0..1`, glowing gold-cyan chest veins + blazing cosmic eyes + upward hair/beard surge), `aegis` (`0..1`, golden-tasseled shield + solar shockwave dome), `masterBolt` (`0..1`, sculptural Cyclopean plasma spear + orbiting fire helices), and `cloakWind` (`0..1`).
3. **180-Degree Staging Rule**: Keep **SCP-682 anchored LEFT** (facing right / rearing up-right) and **Zeus anchored RIGHT** (descending from upper-right sky, pushed right through skyline in Phase 1, then dominating from right/upper-right in Phases 2–3).
4. **Anti-Monochrome Audit**: Verify that **no attack or clash** uses a single solid color—especially the **Master Bolt** (must visibly layer white-gold core + Olympian orange-gold fire helices + electric cyan forks + royal-indigo/violet corona) and **682's Electric-Acid Torrent** (chartreuse-lime + emerald + arterial crimson + black smoke).
