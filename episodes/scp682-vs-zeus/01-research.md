### 1.2 Canon Lore & Sequel Continuation (From Episode #005)
- **Continuity State (Opening Frame)**: Unlike Episode #005 where SCP-682 began in its base breached form, Episode #006 opens with SCP-682 **ALREADY evolved** from its battle with Pegasus: clad in **obsidian-chitin lightning-proof armor** and bearing **massive tattered horror bone-wings**, rampaging across the burning night city avenue after Pegasus broke away to Mount Olympus.
- **Extreme Regeneration & Mass-Loss Survival (Canon)**: SCP-682's defining Keter property is surviving catastrophic physical destruction—up to ~85–87% body mass loss, incineration, and anomalous erasure attempts—and rebuilding from tiny surviving tissue or bone fragments (*SCP-682*; *Experiment Log T-98816-OC108/682*). This directly grounds the Episode #006 Direction 2 cliffhanger: even after Zeus's Master Bolt vaporizes 99.9% of 682's physical body into glowing ash, **one charred bone shard** buried deep in a cracked subway/storm fissure pulses crimson-green and begins regenerating fleshy tendrils.
- **Reactive Energy Absorption & Escalation**: Across termination logs (e.g., SCP-409 crystal immunity, SCP-536 physical-law restructuring, and kinetic/thermal weapons testing), SCP-682 does not merely endure repeat threats—it **metabolizes and weaponizes** them. Because it already adapted to Pegasus's Zeus-channeled thunderbolts in Episode #005, Zeus's opening casual sky-bolt does not hurt 682; instead, 682's grounding armor **absorbs the electrical surge** and triggers an **Overcharged Electric-Acid Titan Surge `(story)`**.
- **Malice & Vocal Profile**: Deep, rasping, guttural bass filled with contempt for all life and divine arrogance alike. Final cliffhanger whisper from the regenerating shard: *"Still... alive."*

### 1.3 Visual Anatomy & Evolution States (For Carbon — Rig `scp682` Extended)
> **IP Safety**: Maintain the original horror-anatomy rig built for Episode #005 (zero copyrighted game models or beluga carcass traces).
- **Starting State (`armor: 1, wings: 1`)**:
  - **Skull & Jaws**: Elongated pale ivory crocodilian skull (`#E8E2D0` highlight, `#8E8570` suture shadow), interlocking gore-stained fangs, hollow nasal ridge, glowing **chartreuse-lime eyes (`#D9FF3F`) with inflamed crimson-orange rims (`#FF3B1F`)**.
  - **Obsidian Lightning-Proof Armor**: Interlocking matte dark obsidian-slate dorsal and shoulder plates (`#1C222B` base, `#4D596B` metallic bevel) laced with pale bone grounding ridges (`#E5DFCC`).
  - **Tattered Horror Bone-Wings**: Towering articulated skeletal wing-fingers (`#DDD5C0`) supporting ragged, translucent venous crimson-black membranes (`#3D0D14` → `#181216`) veined with pulsing toxic lime (`#66FF33`).
  - **Torso & Exposed Ribcage**: Scarred olive-charcoal hide (`#2B3028`) parted over pale ribs (`#DFD8C4`) and glistening crimson muscle (`#99111B`), matted black dorsal mane (`#1B1A1E`).
- **New Episode #006 States (`rig` layers for Carbon)**:
  1. **Overcharged Titan Surge (`overcharge: 1`, Phase 1)**: After absorbing Zeus's casual sky-bolt, 682's bulk swells ~18–25% larger; absorbed **cyan-amber lightning arcs (`#48F0FF` / `#FFAA2B`)** crackle violently through its obsidian plates and merge with **boiling chartreuse-lime HCl veins (`#BFFF2E`)** and **arterial crimson muscle fissures (`#FF2438`)**.
  2. **Shattered / Broken (`shattered: 1`, Phase 2)**: Zeus's **Aegis Shield** solar-gold shockwave rips off 682's tattered bone-wings in a spray of splinters and shatters the obsidian armor plates into flying dark shards, exposing raw glowing ribs and charred crimson flesh.
  3. **Vaporizing / Ash-Disintegration (`vaporize: 0..1`, Phase 3)**: Under the full-power **Master Bolt**, 682's silhouette turns incandescent white-gold-violet at the edges, flaking rapidly into millions of **glowing amber-crimson and indigo-white ash embers** drifting across the night sky.
  4. **Cliffhanger Bone Shard (`shard_regen`, Epilogue)**: Deep inside a wet, cracked subway/storm fissure beneath the quiet dawn city, a **single blackened charred bone shard** (`#1E1B1A` charred crust with `#DFD8C4` cracked ivory core) pulses with a **crimson-green heartbeat glow** (`#FF1F3D` + `#7CFF3B`), sprouts writhing scarlet sinew tendrils, and cracks open a single glowing toxic-acid eye.

### 2.2 Canon Lore & Behavioral Profile
- **Sovereign of Cosmos & Sky**: King of the Olympians, wielder of supreme storm authority (*Nephelegereta*, Cloud-Gatherer; *Keraunios*, Lord of the Thunderbolt; *Aigiochos*, Aegis-Bearer).
- **Combat Temperament (Why Phase 1 → Phase 2 Works)**: Zeus descends in majestic, wrathful pride—expecting a single casual sky-bolt to smite a mortal abomination as easily as an insolent mortal. When SCP-682 **absorbs** that bolt, mutates into an overcharged titan, and slams Zeus through the burning city skyline, Zeus drops all restraint. Like his duel against Typhon (*Theogony* 839–858), he unleashes his true **King of Olympus Overdrive**: lifting the **Aegis Shield** to break the monster's defenses and calling down the **Master Bolt** forged by the Cyclopes.

### 2.3 Colossal Olympian Visual Anatomy Spec (For Carbon — Original Rig `zeus`)
> **Strict Rule**: Zeus MUST look like an awe-inspiring, colossal classical war-king—**muscular, bare-chested, thick silver-white beard, golden laurel crown, billowing storm-mantle**—illuminated by layered **solar-gold, electric sky-cyan, and royal-indigo** spectra (never a flat yellow stick-figure!).
- **Physique**: Broad-shouldered, heavily muscled classical titan-build (`#D9A47C` warm bronze-marble skin midtone, `#F7D4B2` lightning-lit highlight, `#87553B` sculpted muscle shadow) with bare chest crisscrossed by glowing golden-cyan divine veins during `overdrive: 1`.
- **Head, Beard & Crown**: Stern, regal brow; **thick, flowing silver-white beard and windswept hair** (`#F5F8FF` bright lock, `#B6C7E8` storm-blue midtone, `#6B7CA8` deep shadow); crowned with a **burnished golden laurel wreath** (`#FFE47A` → `#D98C1A`).
- **Eyes**: Solid blazing **white-gold irises** (`#FFFDF0` core + `#FFD447` rim) that shift to **white-cyan-indigo cosmic flare** (`#70F3FF` / `#6F4CFF`) in Overdrive.
- **Attire & Storm-Cloak**:
  - Heavy **Olympian gold-trimmed white himation/kilt** (`#F4F2EB` cloth, `#E6A82E` Greek meander/key-pattern border, `#7D8CA8` storm folds) secured by a sun-burst golden belt and bronze-gold greaves/bracers (`#FFD25A` / `#9E6312`).
  - **Billowing Storm-Cloak** (`#1B2A59` deep royal-indigo inner lining, `#3258A8` tempest-blue exterior, `#4CE8FF` crackling cyan hem, `#FFD859` gold lightning embroidery) whipping dynamically in the gale.
- **Divine Regalia (Equip Layers)**:
  - **Aegis Shield (`aegis: 1`)**: Forged circular/convex Olympian war-shield of **burnished solar gold** (`#FFE680` → `#D98B1C`) rimmed with **hundred-tasseled golden fringes** (*Iliad* 2.448) and a **sky-cyan & royal-indigo tempest ring** (`#3EE6FF` / `#2E3DA8`) around a glowing central Gorgon/sunburst boss that radiates blinding shockwaves.
  - **Master Bolt (`masterBolt: 1`)**: Not a thin line—a **jagged, double-ended sculptural spear of solidified Cyclopean lightning** (~2.2× Zeus's height when fully summoned), forged from **blinding white-gold plasma core (`#FFFFFF` / `#FFF5B8`)**, twisted with **molten Olympian solar-fire helices (`#FF9E1F` / `#FFD447`)**, **electric azure-cyan arcs (`#36D6FF`)**, and **deep royal-indigo/violet divine corona (`#6A3DFF`)**.
