[SHOT #15 high, scene domain-city-storm] Golden storm clouds vortex above the shattered skyscrapers; forked sky `lightning` splits the night (`pillar`, `sparks`).
[SHOT #16 low] Colossal thunder `pillar` crashes RIGHT onto the avenue; muscular Zeus lands in a shockwave of gold-cyan dust (`land-hero`, `impact`, `dust`, `shake`).
> Zeus descended in golden *fury*, confronting the winged, armored SCP Six Eight Two.
[SHOT #17 wide] 1v1 establishing: evolved winged armored SCP-682 LEFT vs towering muscular Zeus RIGHT across the burning city avenue.

### BEAT 1: 682 ABSORBS ZEUS'S FIRST BOLT & SURGES
> Zeus hurled a warning *thunderbolt*, expecting instant ash.
[SHOT #18 cu] Zeus rears his muscular right arm back, hurling a bright cyan-gold thunderbolt (`bolt-throw`, `glint`).
[SHOT #19 versus] Whip-pan LEFT: forked `lightning` and sky `beam` slam dead-center into SCP-682 (`impact`, `sparks`).
> Instead, the reptile's evolved *armor* drank the lightning and surged with stolen power.
[SHOT #20 low] SCP-682's obsidian plates crackle with absorbed green-gold electricity (`aura` surge, `pillar`, `story`).
> (scp682's voice, layered guttural roar) Delicious!
[SHOT #21 versus] Whip-pan RIGHT: supercharged SCP-682 launches a storm of ivory bone `spikes` and electrified acid `orbs` straight at Zeus (`story`).
> Supercharged, the monster *slammed* Zeus through the shattered street.

[SHOT #23 cu] Zeus skids backward across the cracked asphalt (`stagger`, `sparks`), cloak whipping in the storm.

### BEAT 2: TWIN THUNDERBOLT BARRAGE & GOD-KING OVERDRIVE

[SHOT #25 versus] Whip-pan LEFT: blinding gold-cyan twin `lightning` spears & crescent `slash` slam straight into SCP-682 (`flash`, `impact`, `sparks`).
