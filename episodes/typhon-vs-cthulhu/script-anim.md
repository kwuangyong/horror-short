# TYPHON vs CTHULHU · script-anim (Battle Spin, sakuga) · Episode #004

Word count (all `>` lines: intros + narration + dialogue + whisper): 237 words. Shots: 60. Estimated length: about 104 s (narrator rate +12%, voice FX deep/blend).
Combo: A2 (fierce back-and-forth) · B2 (kid & fishing fleet pushed to rocky shore, kid watches from afar) · C3 (stars align + emerald eyes under water) · E3 (beam-clash hook) · moonless ocean storm. Layout: Typhon LEFT (x~MX), Cthulhu RIGHT (x~GX), 180-degree rule in every face-off shot.
Typhon design (Hesiod/Apollodorus, not God of War/Immortals): colossal volcanic-ash humanoid torso, hundred dragon/serpent heads erupting from shoulders with fiery eyes, giant obsidian serpent coils below the waist, smoky black wings rimmed with embers. Cthulhu design (Lovecraft 1928, not RPG/board games): pulpy octopus-like head with writhing facial tentacles, wet scaly green-rubber dragon torso, long narrow bat-like wings, prodigious claws, emerald-violet psychic rim light.
