# Script: "DUAL SPIN #001 · VRYKOLAKAS vs CHURCH GRIM" (hook + 2 spins + A/B choice)

> Ngôn ngữ video: tiếng Anh · Giọng: trầm, chậm, thì thầm · Mục tiêu: 95–105 giây.
> **Số từ lời dẫn (chỉ dòng `>`): 231 từ** · Thời lượng ước tính: ~100 giây (kể cả khoảng lặng, thẻ spin, đếm ngược).
> `*word*` = từ khóa đỏ · `[SCENE]` · `[SFX]` · `[UI]` · `[HUD]` · `(…)` = chỉ dẫn đọc (không đọc thành tiếng)

**Clues (đã cài):**
1. Side character: little brother **Nikos sleepwalks**; Grandma Eleni (eighty) gave the rules (~0:13).
2. Rule loophole: **"If it calls, don't answer"** + garlic over the door is **dry as paper** (~0:20). Garlic cannot last the night; a sleepwalker cannot "not answer".
3. The trick: **Grim guards the churchyard, inside the wall only**; seeing Grim = someone dies (~0:36).
→ Correct choice: **B (run to the black dog)** because the garlic is dying, Nikos will answer the call in his sleep, and the only place a guardian can protect you is inside the churchyard wall, 20 steps away. Staying (A) is outside Grim's ground. The "omen" is the fear that makes A tempting.
