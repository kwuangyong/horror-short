# Example script: "THE MA DA" – Vietnam's drowned ghost (hook + A/B choice format)

> Ngôn ngữ video: **tiếng Anh**. Giọng: **trầm, chậm, thì thầm đầy đe dọa** (xem [docs/05-giong-doc-tram.md](../../docs/05-giong-doc-tram.md)).
> Truyền thuyết dân gian Việt Nam (không vướng bản quyền, lạ với khán giả quốc tế). Thời lượng: **~100 giây**, cả hai kết nằm trong 1 video.
> Quy ước: `*word*` = từ khóa tô đỏ trong phụ đề · `[SCENE]` hình ảnh · `[SFX]` âm thanh · `[UI]` đồ họa · `[HUD]` đồng hồ · `(…)` = chỉ dẫn cách đọc cho giọng đọc, KHÔNG đọc thành tiếng.

**Manh mối đã cài** (kiểm tra trước khi sản xuất):
1. Ti is **terrified of water** – never goes near the river (~0:10).
2. Grandpa: it can **copy any voice**, but it can't fake **warmth** (~0:18).
3. The trick: it always uses your **full name**. Family only calls you **"Bean"** (~0:33).
→ At the choice, the voice says "**Minh Tran**" and the kid outside is **soaking wet** → **B** is correct.

---

## 0 · HOOK (0:00–0:03)

[SCENE] 0.7s flash: a bloated white hand slaps against window glass from outside, water streaming. VHS rewind ⏪.
[SFX] Deep impact + tape rewind screech.
[UI] Big text: **"ONLY 4% CHOOSE RIGHT"**

> (whisper, slow) At the end… you *choose*.
> (beat) Choose *wrong*… and you *drown*.

## 1 · SERIES INTRO (0:03–0:05)

[SCENE] Your own series logo (e.g. "GHOST MAP": a red pin drops onto Vietnam). Card: **"FILE #07 · THE MA DA"**.
[SFX] Pin "thunk" + short riser.
[HUD] `GHOST MAP · THE MA DA` · `● 2:47 AM`

## 2 · SETTING (0:05–0:12)

[SCENE] Stilt house over the Mekong, flood season, water lapping at the porch, weak moon → push in on an oil-lamp window.
[SFX] Frogs, water slaps, wind.

> Mekong Delta. Two forty-seven *A.M.*
> Flood *season*. You're sleeping at *Grandpa's*.

[SCENE] Wooden loft, two sleeping mats. Little brother asleep, hugging a pillow, dry clothes.

> Your little brother *Ti* sleeps beside you.
> Ti is *terrified* of water. He never goes near the *river*.

## 3 · RULES (0:12–0:21)

[SCENE] Grandpa at the top of the ladder holding an oil lamp, half his face in shadow.
[UI] Cards pop in: **RULE 1 · NEVER ANSWER** → **RULE 2 · DON'T OPEN THE DOOR** → **RULE 3 · TOUCH THEIR HAND**

> Grandpa gave you three *rules*.
> If something calls from the river… never *answer*.
> Before the rooster *crows*… don't open the *door*.
> It can copy any *voice*. But it can't fake *warmth*.

## 4 · FIRST SIGN (0:21–0:28)

[SCENE] Through a gap in the wall: black water, a head of long hair rises… and sinks.
[SFX] A soft "plop". A distant, warped lullaby.

> Then you *hear* it.
> Someone singing a *lullaby*. Under the *water*.

## 5 · LEGEND + THE TRICK (0:28–0:37)

[SCENE] Grey-blue monochrome, small label "THE LEGEND": a body sinking, bubbles, dozens of hands below.
> People who drown become *Ma Da*.
> To be *free*… they must drag someone down to take their *place*.

[UI] Box "THE TRICK": **CALLS YOUR FULL NAME → IT'S HER**
> And here's the *trick*.
> It always calls your *full* name. Family only calls you *Bean*.

## 6 · ESCALATION (0:37–0:58)

[SCENE] POV lying on the mat. [UI] Meter **"WATER LEVEL"** rising bar by bar.
[SFX] Dripping, creaking wood.

> The water starts to *rise*.
> Through the floorboards. *Ice* cold.

[SCENE] Wet footprints climb the ladder, one rung at a time, and stop at the edge of your mat.
> Wet *footsteps*.
> They stop… right at your *feet*.

[SCENE] You turn: Ti's mat is **empty**, the pillow still dented. Camera shake.
[SFX] One loud heartbeat. Music cuts out.
> You turn to *Ti*.
> His mat… is *empty*.

[SCENE] The wooden door downstairs. [SFX] Three knocks.
> *Knock. Knock. Knock.*
> (child's voice, crying) "Bean… I fell in the *river*… open the door…"

[SCENE] Through the crack: a kid Ti's size, **soaking wet**, hair plastered down, head bowed.
> (child's voice, flatter) "It's so *cold*… open the door… *Minh Tran*."

## 7 · CHOICE POINT (0:58–1:05)

[HUD] Clock **freezes** at 2:59 AM, flashing red.
[SCENE] Split screen.
[UI] Top: **"CHOOSE."** · Red panel **A · OPEN THE DOOR** · Blue panel **B · STAY QUIET, FIND TI** · Countdown **5 → 0**.
[SFX] Heartbeat speeds up with each number, clock ticking.

> Your brother is *freezing* out there.
> Or… he *isn't*.
> Comment *A* or *B*. Five… four… three… two… one.

## 8 · ENDING A – OPEN THE DOOR (1:05–1:20) ☠️

[UI] Stamp **A** slams down, panel B shatters.
[SCENE] You open the door and hug the kid.
> If you chose *A*…
> You open the door. You hold him *tight*.
> His skin is *cold*. Cold as *stone*.

[SCENE] The kid lifts his face: swollen, milky-white eyes, smiling.
[SFX] Huge stinger, rushing water.
> Ti never goes near the *river*.
> And Ti… never calls you *Minh*.

[SCENE] Dozens of hands from the water drag you under. Black. Red text: **"YOUR TURN."**
[SFX] A deep splash, then dead silence.

## 9 · ENDING B – STAY QUIET (1:20–1:40) 🌅

[UI] Rewind ⏪ to the choice. Stamp **B**.
> If you chose *B*…
> You don't *answer*. You look for *Ti*.

[SCENE] Flashlight sweeps under the bed → Ti curled inside the rice jar, **bone dry**, hand over his mouth, crying.
> He's hiding in the *rice jar*.
> Bone *dry*. His hands… *warm*.

[SCENE] The door shakes violently, knocking turns frantic, then stops.
[SFX] Pounding → silence → rooster crow.
> The knocking gets *louder*… then *stops*.
> The rooster *crows*.

[SCENE] Sunrise, palette shifts to warm orange, water recedes. Music softens.
> By morning, the water is *gone*.

[SCENE] Slow push in on the window glass: **a wet handprint… on the INSIDE.** One out-of-tune note.
> But on the window… there's a *wet* handprint.
> On the *inside*.

## 10 · CTA (1:40–1:45)

[SCENE] Fixed series CTA screen (mascot + Follow button being tapped).
[UI] **"DID YOU CHOOSE RIGHT?"**
> The clue was at *ten* seconds. Did you *catch* it?
> Follow… if you want to survive the next *night*.
